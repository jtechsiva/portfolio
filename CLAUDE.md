# Portfolio site

## Source of truth
- The files in `brief/` are the source of truth about me. Read them before any design or content work.
- Never invent anything about me.

## What goes on the page
- The positioning statement in `brief/about.md` is for you, not for the page. Use it to guide the design and the tone of the writing. Never put the statement or its "but not" lines on the page as text.
- The questions and labels in `brief/` are never headings or copy on the page, and neither are my answers about why I'm building this or who it's for. Those are for you. Write the page the way a visitor would read it, in plain words.

## Inspiration
- Images in `inspiration/ideas/` are things I like. Images in `inspiration/anti-patterns/` are things I don't.
- Each image has a note beside it in a text file with the same name. Read the note before you use the image.

## Decisions
- Do not make design decisions for me. When something isn't decided, ask me, and offer options.
- Keep `decisions.md` up to date. At the end of every session, write down what we decided and why.

## Build
- The site is plain HTML and CSS. No frameworks, no build step.

## Git
- Commit and push to GitHub on the `main` branch only when I ask.
